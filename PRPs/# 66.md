## Technical Defect Brief: Playhead Navigation & Transport Logic

**Status:** Open / Functional Regressions
**Component:** Audio Transport Control / Playhead Logic
**Primary Issue:** Inconsistent behavior and playback failure when relocating the playhead during active playback.

---

### **Summary of Defects**

Current playback logic fails to support seamless "scrubbing" or manual playhead relocation. Instead of resuming from the new temporal position, the system triggers unintended track selection changes or transport freezes.

### **Observed Failure States**

The following table categorizes the three distinct failure conditions identified during the session:

| Condition | Action | Resulting Behavior (Defect) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Active Playback (Track 1)** | Relocate Playhead | Playhead freezes; system forces selection to the **subsequent track** (Track 2). Audio stops despite the "Stop" button remaining unengaged. |
| **Post-Freeze Recovery** | Press Play (after above) | Playback initiates from the **absolute beginning** of the next track, rather than the user-selected timestamp. |
| **Active Playback (Track 2+)** | Relocate Playhead | Playhead freezes visually. Track selection remains static, but manual positioning is ignored upon resuming playback. |

---

### **Functional Requirements (Expected Behavior)**

To meet professional audio standards, the transport system must be updated to adhere to the following logic:

* **Temporal Continuity:** Moving the playhead during playback should immediately shift the audio buffer to the new position.
* **State Persistence:** Playback should remain in the "Play" state and continue from the new coordinates without requiring a manual restart.
* **Selection Lock:** Manual playhead movement within a track must not trigger a change in track selection or index.

### **Technical Summary of Logic Gaps**

The system currently experiences a breakdown in synchronization between the **Visual Playhead Position**, the **Active Track Index**, and the **Audio Buffer Offset**. Specifically, when a user interrupts the playback flow via a playhead move, the system defaults to a "Track Start" initialization or an "Index Increment" command rather than a "Seek" command.
