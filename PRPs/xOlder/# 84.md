# Product Requirements Prompt (PRP) — [Status Marker] ✅

## 1. Project Context & Objectives
The Step 3 onboarding tooltip ("Press Play") was recently scaled up for better visibility. However, its increased size has caused it to sit too close to the transport controls (overlapping during its bounce animation) and its horizontal alignment is off (the arrow is pointing too far to the right). The objective is to adjust the absolute positioning of the Step 3 tooltip so it sits comfortably higher and shifts to the left, perfectly targeting the Play button.

## 2. Technical Decisions & Dependencies
* **Target File:** `src/components/TransportControls.tsx`
* **Architectural Action:** Adjust Tailwind CSS positioning utilities (specifically `bottom`/`mb` and `left`/`-translate-x` or `ml`) on the tooltip's wrapper.
* **Design Spec:**
  * **Vertical:** Add enough bottom spacing (e.g., change `mb-2` to `mb-6`, or increase `bottom-X`) so the tooltip's bounce animation never visually intersects with the transport background panel.
  * **Horizontal:** Nudge the element leftwards so the arrow (which is now on the left side of the tooltip) aligns exactly over the center of the Play button (the second button in the transport group).

## 3. Implementation Phases

### Phase 1: Adjust Step 3 Tooltip Positioning — [Status Marker] ✅ Completed
* **Objective:** Refine the vertical clearance and horizontal alignment of the Step 3 pointer.
* **Instructions:**
