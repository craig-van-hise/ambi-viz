## 1. Project Context & Objectives
The 3-step onboarding walkthrough is functional, but requires two UI layout corrections. First, the Step 2 tooltip is currently rendering beneath the main WebGL 3D canvas layer due to a stacking context (`z-index`) issue. Second, the Step 3 tooltip is misaligned—it points vaguely toward the Stop button rather than the Play button. The objective is to elevate the tooltip's z-index and re-align the Step 3 arrow for precise UX.

## 2. Technical Decisions & Dependencies
* **Target Files:** * `src/components/onboarding/AnimatedPointer.tsx`
  * `src/components/TransportControls.tsx`
* **Architectural Action:** * **Z-Index:** Apply a dominant z-index (e.g., `z-50`) to the `AnimatedPointer` component to guarantee it renders above the Three.js canvas.
  * **Alignment/Layout:** Modify the Step 3 tooltip layout so the arrow drops down from the *left* side of the tooltip card. This allows the tooltip to be positioned directly over the Play button while the text naturally flows to the right, preventing visual crowding over the rest of the transport controls.
